    Questions

    You dey ask? We dey answer.

    Will my customers know it's an AI?

    Ada introduces herself as your virtual assistant, and she's honest if anyone asks. Most customers just care that someone picked and helped them quickly. Her voice is warm and natural, not a robot.

    Do I need a new phone number?

    No. Keep your number on your flyers and Instagram bio. You dial a short code once, and only the calls you miss, reject or can't take go to Ada. Pick when you can; Ada picks when you can't.

    What happens when Ada doesn't know the answer?

    She never guesses. She tells the customer you'll call back within the time you choose (e.g. 10 minutes) and pings your WhatsApp. Reply with the answer and Ada calls them back, and remembers it for next time.

    Does it work with MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile?

    Yes. Call forwarding works on all Nigerian networks, and customers can call from any network.

    Can Ada collect payment?

    Yes. After an order, she texts the customer a secure Paystack link (card, transfer or USSD), and the money goes straight to your bank account. You get a WhatsApp message when it's paid.

    What if my minutes finish?

    Top up your wallet like airtime. If you run out completely, Ada keeps picking your calls and takes messages for you, so no customer is ever ignored.
